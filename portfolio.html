N-01

Gate Kiosk

Tablet app for the security desk
Open kiosk ↗
One touch-screen at the factory gate that replaces four registers.
  • Visitor check-in and check-out with QR badge or OTP, invitation verified live.
  • Overtime verification: guard taps the approved OT request, expected OUT time shows, record auto-completes.
  • Fuel and meter-reading entry for company bikes and cars, picked from the staff list.
  • Work-pass attendance for contract workers and face-match support for employees.

N-02

Employee Portal (ESS)

Self-service for every staff member
Open portal ↗
Leave, overtime, visitor invites and other requests from one login, on the phone.
  • Staff raise a request; HR approves from an admin overview; the gate sees it the same minute.
  • Visitor requests show the live "arrived / left" state from the kiosk.
  • Retired a separate host panel by folding it into this portal.

Payroll & HR Admin

N-05

Order-to-Dispatch Flow Tracker

Planned vs actual, step by step
Every order broken into steps with an owner, a trigger and a TAT in working days.
  • Due dates computed forward from the order or backward from the dispatch date.
  • Conditions by category, brand, priority, urgent or on-hold.
  • Planned, actual, status and delay visible per step, so follow-up is a glance, not a call.

How I work

From register to system

Step 1

Sit at the desk

I start where the register sits: the gate, the store, the HR cabin. The form fields come from what people already write by hand.

Step 2

One database

Every module reads the same tables. A visitor approved in the portal is already on the guard's screen.

Step 3

One tap, no training

Big tiles, Hindi labels where needed, no photo uploads or typing that slows a guard down at 9 am.

Step 4

Ship and iterate

Deploy on GitHub Pages the same day, watch it in use, and fix what the floor tells me in the next commit.
